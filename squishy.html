Each character is a real glTF model, and every one of its vertices owns a spring, so a squeeze caves in actual geometry rather than faking it with a texture. Drag sideways to turn a friend around: the yaw stays where you leave it, while a tipped-over friend stands itself back up. Squeeze sounds are synthesised live in the browser — no audio files ship at all, and every character has its own voice. Your squish count, combos, trophies and last-picked friend are saved on this device only.
