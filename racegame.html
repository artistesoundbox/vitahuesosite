ASCII CITY GRAND PRIX

Five aircraft race three laps of a circuit threaded between the blocks of a city that is drawn entirely as text. The city, the road and the racers are real 3D — every frame is rendered tiny, then each texel is turned into a brightness, looked up in a character ramp and stamped out as a glyph, which is why a tower becomes a wall of # and the sky stays empty. Hold the throttle, brake for the chicane, and try not to be the one who parks a starfighter in a wall.

      The five models arrive as 51 MB of glTF; an offline pass collapses them to 249 KB of raw triangles, because
      an ASCII grid gives a racer about 120 characters and would throw the rest away — that pass also centres,
      scales and turns each hull to a known facing. Driving runs on a closed spline: speed decides how much
      steering authority you keep and how hard the corner throws you wide, so a corner taken flat out puts you in
      the wall.
