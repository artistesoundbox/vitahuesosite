Your history — the algorithm's memory

The algorithm runs entirely in your browser: it learns only from what you watch, like, and save here, and narrows the feed the same way recommendation engines do. Scramble wipes its memory. Searches go out live to YouTube through open front-ends — every result is then checked against YouTube itself, and only videos that are verified playable make it onto the table. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the vault feed works even fully offline.
