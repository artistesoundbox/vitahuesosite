Important — please read

Hemisync is a relaxation and meditation aid, created for calm, focus, and rest. It is not medical advice, not therapy, and not a substitute for professional diagnosis, treatment, or counseling of any kind. Nothing on this page treats, cures, or prevents any condition.

Safety: do not listen while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation requiring full attention. If you have epilepsy or a seizure condition, a heart condition, or a pacemaker, consult your doctor before using binaural or rhythmic audio. Keep volume comfortable — never loud.

Speak to Yourself is a private self-reflection tool, not a therapist and not a crisis resource. Everything you write stays on your own device. If you are struggling or in crisis, please reach out to a mental health professional — in the US you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, day or night.

AI mirror: the local reflection never leaves your device. If you choose to chat with the AI mirror, only the words you type in the chat (and your recent check-ins, only if you allow it) travel to our AI service to craft a reply — and they are not stored there.

Individual experiences vary. Use gently, and be kind to yourself.
