THE SAGA

Wash ashore on a living norse island. Walk its village, take wing over its mountains, command a longship across its seas — and hold the walls when the night raid comes.

  • Fly like a bird — dive, glide and trade height for speed
  • Sail the flagship with a rowing crew and a coastal regatta to race
  • Defend the village through night raids and earn your medals
  • Treasure hoards on castle rooftops, lighthouse galleries — and out at sea
  • A living island: day-night cycle, fireflies, birdsong and the tide
