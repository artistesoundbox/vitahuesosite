GAME CONCEPT


Title: A Broken Boot On The Brave


Genre:
Third-Person Shooter






Overview:
A Broken Boot On The Brave is a third-person action shooter set in a dense forest environment. The player controls a lone SWAT officer facing overwhelming numbers of hostile attackers. Using skill, positioning, weapons, and environmental cover such as parked vehicles, the player must survive intense firefights and eliminate waves of enemies.


“These guys got kicked so hard by my Boot  there were equipment failures”


The game blends serious military-style action with the larger-than-life attitude of classic action heroes.


Core Features:
- Third-person shooting
- Large forest map
- Multiple enemy attackers
- Parked vehicles used as cover
- Fast-paced combat
- Survival gameplay
- Weapon pickups
- Action movie atmosphere


Story:
Deep within a hostile forest, a heavily trained SWAT officer finds himself surrounded by enemy forces. Outnumbered and isolated, he refuses to retreat. Instead, he turns the battlefield into a nightmare for anyone brave enough to challenge him.


The enemies think they have trapped their target.


They are wrong.


Taglines:
"One Man. One Forest. One Broken Boot."
"I'm Not Trapped Here With You. You're Trapped Here With Me."
"The Boot Didn't Break Itself."
"Some Soldiers Are Brave. Others Are About To Learn Why."


Tone:
Action-packed
Heroic
Intense
Humorous
Inspired by classic one-man-army action films


Selling Point:
A Broken Boot On The Brave delivers the fantasy of being an unstoppable action hero taking on impossible odds while maintaining a lighthearted sense of humor beneath the action.

THE LEGACY OF THE BOOT


Long before The Boot marched across battlefields, there was another.


A weapon whispered about in terrified households.
A force feared by rebels, troublemakers, and children who thought they could get away with things.


Her name was...


MOMMA CHANCLETA.


Legends say Momma Chancleta never missed.


Distance did not matter.
Corners did not matter.
Excuses definitely did not matter.


Those who heard the mighty *FWAPPP!* of the Chancleta cutting through the air knew their fate had already been decided.


Entire armies surrendered before impact.


Generals abandoned their posts.


Cowards fled.
Heroes prayed.


After decades of maintaining discipline across the known world, Momma Chancleta retired and vanished into legend.


But before leaving, she passed her ancient wisdom to a single heir.


A young boot.


Raised in the sacred ways of intimidation, dramatic entrances, and extreme disappointment, the boot trained endlessly to honor the Chancleta legacy.


Years later, that boot became known as...


THE BOOT.


Unlike ordinary weapons, The Boot does not seek to destroy.


It seeks to embarrass.


Bullets may wound.


Explosions may shake the ground.


But a strike from The Boot fills its victims with such overwhelming shame that many drop their weapons, scream in panic, and run for the nearest exit.


Veteran soldiers have been heard saying:


"I'd rather be shot."


Military historians agree that The Boot is one of the most feared weapons in existence.


Military historians also agree they would rather not discuss why.


For even now, when the battlefield grows silent, some claim they can hear a distant voice carried by the wind...


"YOU BETTER BEHAVE."


And every boot knows exactly whose voice it is.
